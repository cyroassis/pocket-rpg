How the game works

WalkingEvery 200 steps: 5 XP and 1 material. Materials stop at 20 a day; XP never stops. About 1 find in 50 is an item instead (tier 1 to 3, any kind).
Craft5 to 50 materials, in steps of 5. Every 5 is one roll and the best roll wins, so 50 materials give 10× the luck. 2 XP per 5 materials.
Rarity20 tiers. Each tier is 1.6× rarer than the one before. Tier 20 is about 1 in 7,600 with 5 materials.
EquipA tier N item needs level N. You can craft and keep anything.
Levels100, 125, 150, 200, 250, 325, 400, 500, 650, 800 XP, then the same ×10 every 10 levels, with no top level.
Bag20 slots. Salvage gives back 2 materials for every 5 used.
TradeTwo boards side by side. A 60 s window: each hero puts 0 to 3 items on the table and approves. Any change removes the approvals. When both approve, the items swap.

On the board

Tap the hero on the home screen to see the gear being worn. PWR switches to the always-on view (name, steps and footprints) and back. BOOT opens Settings. After the idle time the screen dims to the always-on view, or turns off if that is disabled in Settings. Touch is off on the always-on view; BOOT or PWR bring the normal screen back.

Trading

Add a second board to try a trade: it has its own hero and bag, and the two boards talk over a pretend radio, like two real boards side by side. On both, open Bag and tap Trade.

Tap again to delete the heroes, the bags and the steps.
